Welcome to Webdesk!

Webdesk is a browser-based operating system environment. Here is a quick guide to help you navigate and customize your experience.

Navigating the Desktop

  • Click on any of the app icons on the main screen (Launchers) to start an application.
  • You can drag windows by clicking and holding their title bars. Resize them by dragging the edges. Use the colored buttons in the top right of the title bar to minimize (green), maximize (blue), or close (red) the window.
  • Open and minimized applications appear in the dock at the bottom of the screen. Click an app's icon in the dock to bring it to the front or restore it.

Customization

  • Open the Backgrounds app to upload, set, or export custom wallpapers. Webdesk supports standard images and SVGs.
  • Open the Colors app to change the system theme. You can upload or remove custom JSON-based UI palettes to personalize the interface.

Included Applications

  • A basic command-line interface. Currently, you can test it out using the echo command.
  • A markdown reader that parses and displays local .md files.
